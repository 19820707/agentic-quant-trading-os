# Research Contracts

Prediction research contracts define non-executable payloads for research dossiers,
probability estimates, hypotheses, analyst reports, debate transcripts, signal
candidates, advisory risk review inputs and prediction-market toolkit
governance.

These contracts must not contain credentials, private endpoint data, broker or
exchange secrets, wallet keys, executable orders, `ApprovedOrder` payloads or
live trading authority.

Toolkit governance contracts may classify external tools, strategy patterns,
market-making research policy, liquidity quality and spread risk. They must not
approve dependencies, runtime use, quote publication, inventory rebalancing or
execution.
