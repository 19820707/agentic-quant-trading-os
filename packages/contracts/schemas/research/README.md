# Research Contracts

Prediction research contracts define non-executable payloads for research dossiers,
probability estimates, hypotheses, analyst reports, debate transcripts, signal
candidates, advisory risk review inputs, trend observations and local preflight
research.

These contracts must not contain credentials, private endpoint data, broker or
exchange secrets, wallet keys, executable orders, `ApprovedOrder` payloads or
live trading authority.

Trend and preflight research contracts may describe imbalance, trend state,
trend flips, fail-closed preflight policy, simulation-only PnL and local history.
They must not enable accounts, runtime market access, production checks,
settlement, position switching or execution.
