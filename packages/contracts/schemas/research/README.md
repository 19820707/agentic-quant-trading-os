# Research Contracts

Prediction research contracts define non-executable payloads for research dossiers,
probability estimates, hypotheses, analyst reports, debate transcripts, signal
candidates, advisory risk review inputs and arbitrage observation research.

These contracts must not contain credentials, private endpoint data, broker or
exchange secrets, wallet keys, executable orders, `ApprovedOrder` payloads or
live trading authority.

Arbitrage observation contracts are research-only. They may capture synthetic
two-leg observations, sudden moves, conditional hedge plans, market-round timing,
simulation summaries and audit findings, but they must keep execution disabled.
